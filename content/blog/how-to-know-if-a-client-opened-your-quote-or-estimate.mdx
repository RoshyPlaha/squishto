# How to Know If a Client Opened Your Quote or Estimate

To know if a client opened your quote or estimate, send it as a link instead of an attachment. Save the quote as a PDF, upload it at [squish.to/upload](/upload), and send the short link. The stats page shows total opens, opens in the last 7 days, and each recent open with its time, country and source. You don't need an account for the reader, a CRM or a tracking pixel.

Quotes are a good fit for this because the next step is obvious: the first open tells you when a friendly follow-up lands well. Here is the simple version for freelancers, contractors, tradespeople and small agencies.

## Why quotes and estimates are hard to track

Many invoicing tools can tell you when an invoice was viewed, but quotes and estimates are often sent from a document, a spreadsheet or a plain email. Some invoicing apps don't show a "viewed" status for quotes at all, and users regularly ask for one. Others report only that the **email** was opened, not the quote inside it.

A link fixes that, because every visit to the link is counted no matter which app you used to write the quote.

## Send a quote and see when it's opened

1. **Export the quote as a PDF.** Google Docs, Word, Sheets, Numbers and most quoting tools can download a PDF.
2. **Open [squish.to/upload](/upload)** and choose the file.
3. **Name the link after the client**, for example `smith-kitchen-quote`. Custom names use letters, numbers and hyphens, from 3 to 30 characters.
4. **Upload and copy the short link.** You also get a QR code, handy if you hand a printed quote to someone on site.
5. **Send the link** by email, text or WhatsApp: "Here's the quote for the kitchen: squish.to/smith-kitchen-quote".
6. **Check the stats.** Click **View stats**, or go to squish.to/stats and enter the link name.

## Using the stats to time your follow-up

The stats show:

- **Total opens**
- **Opens in the last 7 days** and your **busiest day**
- **A 14-day chart** of daily opens
- **Recent opens**, each with the time, country and source (for example WhatsApp, Direct or an email app)

Some practical ways to read them:

- **Opened the same day:** they have seen it, so a short "any questions on the quote?" the next morning is well timed.
- **Opened several times over a few days:** they are probably comparing or sharing it. Offer a quick call.
- **No opens after a couple of days:** the message may have been missed. Resend the link or try another channel.

These are guides, not rules. You know your client best.

## Honest limits

- **Stats are per link, not per person.** If a couple both open the link, you see two opens but not who. To tell people apart, upload one copy per recipient (`smith-kitchen-quote`, `jones-kitchen-quote`). See [one link per recipient](/blog/one-link-per-recipient-track-who-opened-your-proposal).
- **Not every open is a human.** Link previews in chat apps and email security scanners can load a link automatically, and squish.to counts every visit. An open within seconds of sending may be a preview. An open hours later is far more likely to be a real reader.
- **An open is a visit, not a read.** squish.to doesn't show which pages were viewed or for how long.
- **10MB file cap** per upload.
- **Files stay live for 30 days.** If your client takes longer to decide, upload again and send the new link, or tell them to download their copy.
- **You can't check an attachment after the fact.** If you already sent the quote as a file, send the link version and you will see opens from then on.

## FAQ

**How do I know if a client opened my quote?**
Send the quote as a link instead of an attachment. The link's stats page shows each open with time, country and source.

**Can I track an estimate without invoicing software?**
Yes. Export it as a PDF, upload it at squish.to, and send the short link. No account is needed for you or the client.

**Does the client need to sign up to see it?**
No. They tap the link and the PDF opens in their browser.

**Can I see which person opened it?**
Not directly, because stats are per link. Upload one copy per client to tell them apart.

**Is an open the same as the client reading it?**
No. It means the link was visited. Previews and scanners can add opens that no person made.

**How big can the file be, and how long does it stay online?**
Up to 10MB, and uploaded files stay live for 30 days.

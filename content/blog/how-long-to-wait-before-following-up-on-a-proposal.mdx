# How Long to Wait Before Following Up on a Proposal

Wait two to three business days after sending a proposal, then follow up. If you sent it as a [squish.to](/upload) link, check the stats page first. A recent open means a short, friendly check-in is well timed. No opens yet means a quick "did this reach you?" is better than a pitch. The stats turn a guess into a decision.

The two-to-three-day figure is a common rule of thumb, not a law. Your sales cycle, the size of the deal and the person's calendar all matter, so treat it as a starting point.

## A simple follow-up timeline

1. **Day 0: send one link per recipient.** Upload a copy per person at [squish.to/upload](/upload) and give each a clear name like `acme-maria`. The method is covered in [track who opened your proposal with one link per recipient](/blog/one-link-per-recipient-track-who-opened-your-proposal).
2. **Day 1: peek, don't pounce.** A first open is encouraging, but it is not a reason to call yet. People often skim first and read properly later.
3. **Day 2 to 3: check the stats, then decide.** Use the table below.
4. **Day 5 to 7: a second, lighter nudge** if you have heard nothing, whatever the stats say.

## What the stats suggest

squish.to shows total opens, opens in the last 7 days, your busiest day, a 14-day chart, and the most recent opens with time, source and country. Here is how to read them for timing:

| What you see | A sensible move |
| --- | --- |
| No opens after 2 to 3 days | Send a short "did the link reach you?" note, maybe by another channel |
| One open, a day or two ago | Give it a little longer, or send a light "any questions so far?" |
| A second open on a later day | Good moment to follow up. They came back to it. |
| Several opens across a few days | Offer a call. It is clearly being discussed |
| Opens from a new country or source | Probably forwarded. Ask who else is looking so you can answer their questions |
| Activity, then silence for a week | Re-send with a fresh angle or a small update |

The busiest day is a handy hint for when this reader tends to look at things, though with one recipient it is a small sample. Don't over-read it.

## Keep the tone helpful

The goal is to be useful at the right moment, not to announce that you saw them open it. "Happy to walk through any part of this" lands better than "I see you looked at it Tuesday." You can use the timing without mentioning it.

## Honest limits

Open stats are a timing aid, not proof of interest. Know what they can and cannot tell you:

- **Stats are per link, not per person.** One upload per recipient is how you keep them apart. A forwarded link looks like its original recipient.
- **Link previews and email scanners can add opens that no human made.** An open a second or two after you hit send is often a machine, so wait for a later one before reading anything into it.
- **The recent opens list shows the latest few opens,** so for a busy link rely on the totals and the 14-day chart for the bigger picture.
- **Uploads are capped at 10MB and expire after 30 days.** For a long sales cycle, upload again and send a fresh link when the old one is nearing expiry.
- **squish.to counts opens.** It does not show which pages were read or for how long.

For the wider picture on what counts as an open, [how to know if someone opened your PDF attachment](/blog/how-to-know-if-someone-opened-your-pdf-attachment) covers read receipts versus link tracking.

## FAQ

**How long should I wait before following up on a proposal?**
Two to three business days is a good default. Use the open stats to adjust: follow up sooner after a repeat open, and send a simple "did this reach you?" if there are no opens.

**Should I follow up if they opened it but did not reply?**
Yes, gently. An open means the link reached a person or a scanner, so a short, helpful note is welcome. A second open on a different day is a stronger cue than a single one.

**What if my proposal link shows no opens?**
Check the link was sent to the right address, then ask whether it arrived. It may be in spam, or they may simply not have got to it yet.

**Can I tell who opened it?**
Not from a single shared link. Stats are per link, so upload one copy per recipient and the link name tells you who you sent it to.

**Does the link keep working for a long deal?**
Uploaded files expire after 30 days. Re-upload and send a fresh link if your cycle runs longer.

# How Long to Wait Before Following Up on a Proposal (Use the Open Stats to Time It)

Wait about 3 to 5 business days after sending a proposal, then adjust using what you can see. If the link was opened, a day or two after a second open is a good moment to check in. If it was never opened, follow up sooner, because the problem may be delivery rather than interest. If you send the proposal as a [squish.to](/upload) link, the stats page shows you which of those cases you are in.

Most follow-up advice online gives a fixed number of days because the sender cannot see anything. This post shows how to swap the guess for a few clear signals.

## The default when you can't see anything

If you attached a PDF and have no way to tell whether it was opened, 3 to 5 business days is a sensible default. It is long enough that the reader has had time to look, and short enough that you are still fresh in their mind. If the client gave you a decision date, send your first message one or two days before it, not after.

## What changes when you can see opens

Upload the proposal at [squish.to/upload](/upload), send the link instead of an attachment, and read the link's stats at [squish.to/stats](/stats). Each stats page shows total opens, opens in the last 7 days, your busiest day, a 14-day chart and a list of recent opens with time, source and country. Here is how to use that:

| What you see | Likely situation | Suggested timing |
|---|---|---|
| No opens after a day or two | It may not have landed, or it is buried | Check in soon, and ask if it arrived. Resend the link if needed. |
| One open, then quiet | Skimmed, or a preview | Wait the usual 3 to 5 business days. |
| Two or more opens, spread over different days | Someone is coming back to it | Follow up within a day or two. |
| Opens from a new source or country | Probably forwarded to someone else | A good time to offer a short call with the wider group. |
| Opens clustered right before a deadline | Decision is being prepared | Be available and keep the message short. |

None of these are guarantees. They are prompts for a friendly message, not proof of anything.

## A simple rhythm

1. **Send one link per recipient.** Upload a copy per person with its own link name, like `globex-priya`. That way each stats page maps to one conversation. The full method is in [track who opened your proposal with one link per recipient](/blog/one-link-per-recipient-track-who-opened-your-proposal).
2. **Check the next morning.** Look for an open. If there is none, a quick "did that come through?" is usually welcome.
3. **Check again at day 3.** A repeat open means follow up now. A single open means wait a little longer.
4. **Follow up with something useful.** Offer an answer to a likely question, a revised option or a short call. Keep it light.
5. **Re-upload if the deal runs long.** Uploaded files stay live for 30 days, so for a longer sales cycle upload again and send a fresh link.

## Read the numbers honestly

- **Stats are per link, not per person.** The link name tells you who you sent it to, not who opened it. If it was forwarded, the opens look like they came from the original recipient.
- **Link previews and email scanners can add opens that no human made.** An open within a second or two of sending is often a scanner or chat app preview, so treat the first open as a maybe and a repeat open as the better sign.
- **No page-level detail.** squish.to counts opens. It does not show which pages were read or for how long.
- **Files are capped at 10MB** and expire after 30 days.

For a deeper look at what counts as an open, the guide on [how to know if someone opened your PDF attachment](/blog/how-to-know-if-someone-opened-your-pdf-attachment) compares read receipts with link tracking. Dedicated proposal tools such as PandaDoc or Proposify add things squish.to does not, like page-level engagement and e-signature, so pick those if you need them. For a quick "is it time to follow up?" the open stats cover a lot.

## FAQ

**How long should I wait before following up on a proposal?**
About 3 to 5 business days is a common default. If you can see opens, follow up sooner after a repeat open, and sooner still if it was never opened.

**Should I follow up if the proposal was opened but I got no reply?**
Yes, gently. Wait a day or two after a repeat open, and offer something useful rather than just asking for an answer.

**What if the proposal was never opened?**
Treat it as a delivery question first. Ask whether it arrived and resend the link.

**Can I tell who opened the link?**
Not exactly. Stats are per link, so send one link per recipient and read each stats page as one person.

**Do I need an account to see the stats?**
No. Uploads and stats work without signing up. Enter the link name at squish.to/stats.

# Share a Product Catalogue or Price List as a Short Link

To share a product catalogue or price list as a short link, keep the file in Google Drive or Dropbox, set it to view-only for anyone with the link, then paste that link into [squish.to](/) and choose a name like `acme-catalogue`. Send the short link in chats, status updates, bios and print. When your prices change, replace the file in place and the same short link keeps working.

That last part is the point of this post. A catalogue changes every season, and a link you have printed or pinned should not.

## Why a short link suits a catalogue

A catalogue link gets reused far more than most links. It goes into chat messages, social bios, email signatures, flyers and QR codes, often for months. That makes three things matter:

- **It should be readable.** A long `drive.google.com/file/d/...` address looks like a mess in a chat and is impossible to say out loud. `squish.to/acme-catalogue` is easy to read, type and remember.
- **It should say what it is.** People are more comfortable tapping a link whose name tells them what is behind it.
- **It should survive updates.** You will fix a price or add a product line. The link should not change when you do.

## Step by step

1. **Put the catalogue in Drive.** Export it as a PDF and upload it to Google Drive. A PDF is the safest format for a catalogue, since it looks the same on every phone.
2. **Set the access.** In Drive, choose Share, then under General access pick "Anyone with the link" and set the role to Viewer. Copy the link. Menu labels can shift between app versions, and Google Workspace admins can limit these options, so check what your account offers.
3. **Shorten it.** Paste the Drive link into [squish.to](/) and enter a custom name of 3 to 30 characters, using letters, numbers and hyphens. Names that work well: `brand-catalogue`, `brand-prices-2027`, `brand-menu`.
4. **Share the short link** wherever your customers are. Every squish.to link also comes with a QR code, handy for a market stall or a printed flyer. There is more in [QR codes for every link](/blog/qr-codes-for-every-link).
5. **Check the stats** at [squish.to/stats](/stats) to see total opens, the last 7 days, your busiest day, a 14-day chart and recent opens with source and country.

Dropbox works the same way: create a view-only shared link to the file, then shorten it. Our [Dropbox guide](/blog/how-to-shorten-a-dropbox-link) covers the setting to check first.

## Updating the catalogue without changing the link

Because the short link points at your Drive link, you only need the Drive link to stay the same. For an uploaded file such as a PDF, Drive can do that:

1. Right-click the file in Drive and open File information, then Manage versions.
2. Choose Upload new version and pick the updated PDF.

The file keeps its ID, so the Drive link, its sharing settings and your squish.to short link all carry on working, and customers see the new version. This is covered in Google's Drive help on file versions, and it applies to uploaded files like PDFs and images, not to Google Docs or Sheets, which have their own version history. Menus can differ between plans, so check yours before a big update.

If you would rather start fresh each season, name the link for the season, like `brand-prices-spring`, and keep the old one until the season ends.

## A note on who can open it

Shortening only changes how the link looks. Who can open the file is controlled by Drive or Dropbox, not by squish.to. "Anyone with the link" means anyone who has the link can view it, so it suits a public catalogue and not a private wholesale price list. For a trade-only price list, restrict access in Drive to named people instead.

squish.to is not connected to your Google or Dropbox account. It just stores the link you paste.

## What about a WhatsApp Business catalogue?

If you use WhatsApp Business, its built-in catalogue lets customers browse products inside the app, and it has its own share link. That is a good fit for browsing individual items. A PDF catalogue or price list in Drive suits a different job: a single document you can also use on social media, in email and in print. Many small businesses use both.

## Honest limits

- **Opens are not people.** Chat apps and link previews can fetch a link before anyone taps it, so a few opens may not be human. Treat small numbers as rough.
- **Stats are per link.** If you want to know which channel works, make one short link per channel, such as `brand-catalogue-ig` and `brand-catalogue-wa`.
- **Don't let Drive limits surprise you.** A very popular file can hit Google's download or view limits for a while. If that happens, check Drive's help for the current rules.

If you want a PDF catalogue that you can track more closely, you can also upload it directly at [squish.to/upload](/upload). Uploads are capped at 10MB and expire after 30 days, so they suit a time-limited price list better than a permanent catalogue. ## FAQ

**How do I share a product catalogue as a link?**
Upload the PDF to Google Drive or Dropbox, set it to view-only for anyone with the link, then shorten that link with a clear name at squish.to.

**Can I update the catalogue without changing the link?**
Yes for an uploaded file in Drive. Use Manage versions and Upload new version, and the Drive link stays the same, so your short link does too.

**Will customers need a Google account?**
Not when the file is set to "Anyone with the link". They can open it without signing in.

**Can I see how many people opened my catalogue?**
Yes. The stats page shows total opens, the last 7 days, the busiest day, a 14-day chart and recent opens. Previews can add some opens that are not people.

**Is squish.to connected to my Drive account?**
No. It is a link shortener, so Drive still controls who can open the file.

If you've ever set up a SignUpGenius for a class snack schedule, a volunteer shift, or a supply drive, you've seen the link it hands you by default: something like `signupgenius.com/go/10C0D49ACA62BA5FF2-back23`. That's fine to click. It's not fine to read off a flyer, retype from a printed newsletter, or say out loud at back-to-school night.

SignUpGenius knows this. It even sells the fix - a custom SignUpGenius link where you pick your own ending, like `signupgenius.com/go/fall-bake-sale`, instead of the auto-generated string. There's just one catch: that feature is locked to the **Platinum plan ($59.99/month, or $539.89/year) and Enterprise**. Free, Silver, and Gold - the plans most PTA parents, teachers, and youth-sports coordinators are actually on - don't get it.

That's a real gap for exactly the people who need it most. Nobody organizing a volunteer snack rotation is paying $540 a year for the privilege of a shorter link. But they're also the ones most likely to be sharing that link somewhere unforgiving of long, random URLs: a paper flyer sent home in a backpack, a class group chat, a printed program, or read out loud at a PTA meeting.

## The free fix

You don't need to upgrade your SignUpGenius plan to solve this. Take the long link SignUpGenius gives you and run it through [squish.to](https://squish.to) instead:

1. Copy your sign-up's full URL from the **Share** tab in SignUpGenius.
2. Paste it into squish.to.
3. Pick your own ending - `squish.to/fall-bake-sale`, `squish.to/winter-concert-volunteers`, `squish.to/supply-drive` - instead of letting it generate a random one.

No account required. No plan to upgrade. The link works exactly like the SignUpGenius one - it just doesn't look like a password.

## Where this actually matters

A readable link isn't a nice-to-have here, it's the whole point. Think about where these sign-up links actually travel:

- **Printed flyers and newsletters.** A parent has to type the link in by hand from a piece of paper. `squish.to/spring-fling` survives that. `10C0D49ACA62BA5FF2-back23` does not.
- **Read aloud.** Sign-up links for school events get announced at pickup, mentioned in the PA announcements, or read out at a meeting - the exact scenario where letter-by-letter random strings fall apart. If you want to go further on this, we've written a separate [guide to building a link you can say out loud](/blog/short-links-you-can-say-out-loud) that covers the specific letters and number combos worth avoiding.
- **Group chats and email threads.** A memorable ending is easier for a parent to recognize and re-find later, scrolling back through a busy class thread, than a string of random characters that looks identical to every other link in the chat.

## A quick, honest comparison

To be clear about what you're getting and not getting: SignUpGenius's Platinum plan isn't just paying for a custom link - it also unlocks things like advanced reminders, reporting, and removing ads, which may well be worth it if you're running sign-ups constantly. This isn't a "SignUpGenius is bad" post. It's a specific, narrow gap: if the *only* thing standing between you and a readable sign-up link is the plan you're on, you don't have to pay for it. A free shortener closes that one gap completely.

One thing worth knowing before you pick an ending: squish.to custom slugs run 3-30 characters, using letters, numbers, and hyphens - no underscores - and they're case-sensitive, so keep it lowercase to avoid any confusion later.

## The takeaway

SignUpGenius's own blog calls a long URL "cluttered" and prone to getting cut off in texts and printouts - and then puts the fix behind a $540/year plan. If you're on Free, Silver, or Gold and you're sending a sign-up link anywhere it needs to be typed, read, or remembered, shorten it yourself first. It takes less time than writing the flyer.
